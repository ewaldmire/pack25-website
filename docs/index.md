# Mahomet Cub Scout Pack 25

We're the Cub Scout pack serving Mahomet, Illinois. We meet regularly for
fun, adventure, and learning — building character, citizenship, and
confidence in youth.

## General Info

- **Pack Meeting** — Second Monday of every month, 6:30 PM @ Lincoln Trail Elementary
- **Den Meetings** - Meet 1 Monday per month, see [Calendar](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1j6GQlqhVlV4vXsdT0PjQYu6UFg10sqST7merEAVw3II) for specific dates
- **Popcorn Fundraiser** — kicks off in September
