Pack 25 Swim Adventure¶

When: Monday, August 24th — Check-in at 6:00 PM, tests start at 6:30 PM

Where: Sholem Aquatic Center

RSVP: Sign up on this sheet

Rain Date

Our rain date is August 31st. We'll send a pack-wide email no later than 5 PM on Monday, August 24th if we need to reschedule.

What to Bring¶

  • Completed Annual Health and Medical Form — you'll turn this in to get your buddy tag.
  • Towel and other swimming accessories (optional)
  • Dry clothes in case you get chilly

What to Expect¶

This experience enables our Cub Scouts to earn the elective swimming adventure for their rank. We will swim in areas designated by swimming ability and always with our buddy. A hallmark of the summer camp pool experience is the BUDDY CHECK — everyone gets out of the water and sits on the edge of the pool while staff confirms our safety by reviewing pairs and making sure they're swimming in the appropriate area.

Station 1: Swim Classification & Entry/Exit¶

Administer swim tests and practice safe water entry/exit strategies.

Classification Requirement / Test Standard Target Ranks
Non-Swimmer Did not attempt or complete test. Activity restricted to shallow water (waist-deep or less). Lions & Tigers (water entry/comfort)
Beginner Jump feet-first into deep water, swim 25 feet, turn, and return to start. Wolves & Bears
Swimmer Jump feet-first into deep water; swim 75 yards using strong strokes, plus 25 yards using a restful backstroke (100 yards total); rest on back to show floating ability. Bears, Webelos, AOL

Station 2: Relay & Skill Instruction¶

  • Lions & Tigers: Kickboard skills and blowing bubbles.
  • Wolves: Basic swimming stroke development and jumping feet-first into chest-deep water.
  • Bears, Webelos & AOL: Treading water, self-rescue, and reach/throw rescues.

Station 3: Free Swim & Buddy System¶

Use the Buddy System with regular Buddy Checks called by leaders for 30 minutes.

Siblings & Parents¶

Cub Scouting is for families! To participate, please bring a completed medical form. The staff at Sholem will designate a separate area for your free swim unless you are serving as the buddy for your Cub Scout.

Medical Forms¶

Per Scouting America, anyone participating must turn in a medical form. A pro tip is to fill this out as a PDF so you can make minor updates each year. Download the Medical Form.
